# Word data attribution

New entries derived from Wiktionary, normalized solution/guess lists, editorial meanings and their adapted selection are provided under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Attribute Wiktionary contributors and link to the source entries; `sourceWord` preserves the original entry title. See `sources.json` for the extraction snapshot, download, license evidence, retrieval date and changes. Each entry can be visited at https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/{sourceWord} and its history provides author attribution. No quotations, images or examples from third-party dictionary references were imported.

FrequencyWords content is CC BY-SA 4.0; its corpus frequencies informed selection, not definitions. Code's licensing is separate from lexical data; this notice does not relicense existing application code.

The inherited 145 Jathr entries had no per-word dictionary citations or explicit data license. They are honestly attributed to their original repository revision, not retrospectively claimed as Wiktionary-derived. To respect the instruction not to edit the original 120 entries, their source IDs are in `legacy-sources.json`. Their exact objects and order are regression-tested. `jathir-review.json` identifies the newly curated entries; patterns, inferred roots, meanings and difficulty were editorially reviewed with language knowledge and dictionary headwords, not certified by a lexicographer. Existing fields/difficulty were intentionally retained even where they differ from the new rubric.
